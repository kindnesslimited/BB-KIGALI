Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how B&B Kigali 89.7 FM ("BB FM Kigali", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile app and website ("Service"). We keep it short because we collect only what we need to run the app.

1. What we collect

  • Phone number — for one-time password (OTP) sign-in via SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Apple / Google account identifier & email — only if you choose Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google.
  • Display name — optional, only if you set it in Profile.
  • Payment reference & status — we never see or store your card number or MoMo PIN. Stripe, PayPal, and BeSoft (MTN MoMo) process the payment on their own secure infrastructure. We only store the transaction reference, amount, currency, provider, status, and timestamp for accounting and to unlock your subscription/VOD.
  • Device diagnostics — anonymised error logs, no advertising IDs, no location tracking.

2. How we use it

  • Deliver live radio, videos, and podcasts.
  • Verify your phone number and keep you signed in.
  • Process subscription and pay-per-video payments.
  • Send occasional service messages (never marketing without consent).
  • Respect content licensing (screen-recording protection).

3. Third-party services

  • Stripe — card payments — stripe.com/privacy
  • PayPal — card / PayPal — paypal.com/privacy
  • BeSoft Pay — MTN Mobile Money — besoft.info
  • Route Mobile / Nostress — SMS & WhatsApp OTP delivery.
  • YouTube — video hosting for on-demand content.
  • Apple & Google — Sign in with Apple / Google.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and content history while your account is active. Payment records are retained for tax/accounting for up to 7 years (anonymised after account deletion). OTP challenges expire in 5 minutes and are then discarded.

5. Your rights & account deletion

You can:

  • Update your display name any time in Profile → Edit.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, sessions, and unlocked content; your payment records are anonymised and retained only for legal accounting purposes.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing privacy@bbkigali.com.

6. Data security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS. Payment credentials never touch our servers. Access to our infrastructure is restricted to authorised staff.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the most recent version.
